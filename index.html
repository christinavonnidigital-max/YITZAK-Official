Developing Competence. Enabling Compliance.

Building competence. Driving business excellence.

Yitzak is a professional training and capability-development company that helps organisations build knowledge, competence, and confidence through internationally aligned programmes. We work across many industries to develop capable people and strengthen management systems. Training sits at the heart of everything we do, supported by consulting and certification guidance that helps clients apply what they learn.

Our services

  • Professional Training: Practical, instructor-led training across management systems and industry disciplines — Yitzak programmes, FoodChain ID courses, corporate and in-house delivery, and customised learning.
  • Consulting & Advisory: Gap assessments, management system development, documentation support, and internal audits that turn learning into working systems.
  • Certification Support: Guidance from scope review and audit readiness through to a valid certificate, delivered through our partnership with FoodChain ID and its accredited certification bodies.

Areas of expertise

  • Food Safety: HACCP, FSSC 22000, BRCGS, Food Defence, Food Fraud, GMP, PCQI, Food Safety Culture.
  • Quality Management: ISO 9001, Internal Auditing, Lead Auditor, CAPA, Supplier Auditing, Documentation Control.
  • Environmental Management: ISO 14001, EMS, Climate Change Integration, Environmental Compliance.
  • Occupational Health & Safety: ISO 45001, Hazard ID & Risk Assessment, Emergency Preparedness, Workplace Safety.
  • Integrated Management Systems: IMS Implementation, IMS Internal Auditor, Lead Auditor, Business Process Integration.

Industries we serve

Food manufacturing, beverage production, agriculture, hospitality, manufacturing, pharmaceuticals, logistics and warehousing, construction, retail, healthcare, professional services, government and public sector, SMEs, and large enterprises.
